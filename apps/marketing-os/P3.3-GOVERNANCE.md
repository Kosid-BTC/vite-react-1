# P3.3 Lead/Sales Intelligence governance

P3.3 may summarize only first-party observed lead and sales evidence scoped to one workspace and lead. It must not infer hidden intent, fabricate events, claim causal impact, or invent conversion/revenue values.

Stage is a deterministic evidence label, not a probability. Automated lead scoring is not authorized in this slice: `score` remains `null` and `scoreReason` remains `SCORING_NOT_AUTHORIZED` until an owner-approved scoring policy and validated evidence exist.

Every assessment must retain evidence references, reject cross-workspace or cross-lead inputs, require human review, and remain non-executable. It cannot publish, contact leads, change CRM state, mutate budget, deploy, or authorize production actions.

Evidence maturity is `INSUFFICIENT` with no evidence, `OBSERVED` with one observed source class, and `CORROBORATED` only when at least two distinct first-party source classes support the assessment. Corroboration is not causation.
