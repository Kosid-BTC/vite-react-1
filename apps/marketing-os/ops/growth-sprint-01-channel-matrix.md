# CEO AI Thailand — Growth Sprint 01 Channel Matrix

| Channel | Primary format | Campaign fit | Funnel stage | Primary KPI |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook | Short video + static/carousel | Ads Dependency / Pricing | Awareness → Consideration | Landing visits / CTA clicks |
| TikTok | 8–20s short video | Ads Dependency / AI Team | Awareness | Qualified profile/site visits |
| YouTube Shorts | Short video | Pricing / AI Team / Proof | Awareness → Consideration | Site clicks / engaged viewers |
| Instagram Reels | Short video | Ads Dependency / AI Team | Awareness | CTA clicks |
| Instagram Carousel | Educational cards | Pricing / Proof | Consideration | Saves / landing clicks |
| LinkedIn | Founder POV / framework | AI Team / Proof | Consideration → Lead | Qualified leads |

## Reuse rule
One core idea may be adapted to multiple channels, but hook, caption length, CTA, and thumbnail/title must be native to the channel.

## Evidence rule
Do not judge winners from reach alone. A winner must improve at least one downstream metric: CTA click, signup, workspace activation, first-value completion, qualified lead, or conversion.

## Stop rule
If a format repeatedly produces reach but no measurable downstream movement, stop or rewrite the hook/offer rather than increasing volume.
