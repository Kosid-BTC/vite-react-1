Primary goal: drive qualified Thai SME owners from content to signup, workspace activation, and measurable first value.

## Day 7 — Weekly Winner Loop
Asset: weekly recap / transparent experiment update
Hook: “สัปดาห์นี้อะไรทำให้คนสมัครจริง ไม่ใช่แค่ดู?”
Proof angle: report only evidence-backed winner/loser signals.
CTA: ทดลองระบบและสร้าง experiment ของธุรกิจตัวเอง
Funnel stage: Decision
Primary KPI: qualified signup + activation

## Daily Publishing Rule
For every asset record:
- campaign
- audience
- funnel stage
- hook
- CTA
- destination URL
- UTM source / medium / campaign / content
- publish timestamp
- evidence source

Do not mark a winner from views alone. Winner requires downstream evidence: signup, activation, lead, or conversion.
