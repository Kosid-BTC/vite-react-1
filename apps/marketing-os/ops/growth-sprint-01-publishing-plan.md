# CEO AI Thailand — Growth Sprint 01 Publishing Plan

## Objective
Move from production-ready software to measurable market execution for Thai SMEs.

## Channels
- Facebook: educational posts, founder POV, retargetable traffic
- TikTok: 8–20 second problem/insight clips with one CTA
- YouTube Shorts: reuse best short-form creative with stronger title/description
- Instagram Reels: short-form reuse plus carousel summaries
- LinkedIn: SME/management/AI operations perspective

## Publishing cadence
- Daily: 1 primary short-form post
- 3x/week: 1 supporting static/carousel post
- 2x/week: founder/CEO point-of-view post
- 1x/week: conversion post focused on signup/demo/first-value action

## Content pillars
1. Ads Dependency — reduce dependence on paid acquisition
2. Pricing & Margin — convert pricing mistakes into business insights
3. AI Team for Thai SME — position CEO AI Thailand as an operating system, not just a chatbot
4. Proof / Workflow — show concrete before→after business workflows

## CTA hierarchy
1. Primary: Start free / enter CEO AI Thailand
2. Secondary: Learn the framework / calculator / business diagnostic
3. Retargeting: Return to activate workspace and complete first-value action

## Publishing rule
Every post must map to exactly one campaign, one funnel stage, one CTA, and one UTM campaign name. No untracked publishing.

## Fast-track operating rule
Do not block publishing for non-critical design perfection. Ship evidence-generating content first, then iterate from conversion evidence.
