# CEO AI Thailand — Growth Sprint 01 Measurement Loop

## Measurement chain
Content → Landing → CTA → Signup → Workspace activation → First value → Qualified lead → Conversion.

## Required dimensions
- campaign
- channel
- content_id
- creative_format
- hook
- CTA
- UTM source / medium / campaign / content
- publish time

## Daily review
For each published asset record:
- impressions/reach (context only)
- landing visits
- CTA clicks
- signup starts/completes
- workspace activations
- first-value completions
- qualified leads
- conversions

## Decision rules
### SCALE
Scale only when downstream evidence improves, especially signup, activation, first value, qualified lead, or conversion.

### ITERATE
Iterate when attention exists but downstream movement is weak. Change one variable at a time: hook, proof, CTA, offer, or landing-message match.

### STOP
Stop when repeated executions create attention without measurable downstream value, or when CAC/effort is structurally unattractive versus alternatives.

## Evidence integrity
If a metric is not connected or verified, display `UNAVAILABLE`; never infer a performance number.

## Sprint success definition
Growth Sprint 01 is successful when the team can identify at least one repeatable acquisition→activation path with attributable evidence, not merely high views.
